01 · OPT Digital

Sales video + ad

Type
Long-form sales video and short-form ad
Role
Visual direction, edit, structure, pacing, motion graphics, sound design, colour and captions
Sales video · 16:9

The brief

OPT Digital had never had a sales video. They needed one that looked like it belonged to their brand from the first frame.

What I did

I built the video's visual direction from their website, using their colours and fonts to design every animation and graphic. Then I carried the same style into a short-form ad, so both pieces feel like one brand.

Creative decision

Instead of bringing in a new look, I let their existing brand lead. A viewer who lands on their website after watching sees the same colours, type and feel, so the video and site build trust together.
